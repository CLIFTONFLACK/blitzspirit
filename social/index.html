Section 07 — Where the line is

Guardrails

Straight from the Brand Book's anti-pattern list, with the social-specific ones added. Check every post against it before it goes out.

  • We do not recruitNo "join", no "fight back", no "the resistance". The brand implies defiance; it never issues instructions. Anything that reads as a call to action about anything other than buying a shirt is out.
  • Not party political, not activist, not victimhood-drivenStraight from the "We Are Not" list. No parties, politicians, campaigns, current affairs, or grievance framing. Audience is 30–60 across class and political leaning — the moment the brand picks a side it loses half of them.
  • Banned wordsSystem. Deploy. Weapon. Resistance. Revolution. Uniform. Also avoid surrender, armoury, conscription, arsenal. Cosy period texture is welcome instead — Home Guard, Quartermaster's, Dispatched, Field Manual.
  • Never explain the jokeNo clarifying, no adding context, no backtracking in the comments. If a line needs a follow-up post to explain it, the line was wrong.
  • Claims must be evidencedAnything tagged red on this page is a real-world claim — price, stock, limited runs, customer messages. UK advertising rules apply to organic posts too, not just paid.
  • Paid social is narrower stillCampaign-line led, one line, product or graphic carries it. Target on interests — British heritage, independent brands, history, dry humour. Never target political categories. Run Eased register in paid; keep House organic.
  • No people in brand imageryFixed rule across the brand world. No models, no hands, no wrists. Flat lay or ghost mannequin on concrete, steel, weathered surface. UGC is the one exception and it lives in stories, not the feed.
  • Hashtags: zero to twoTargeted only. No carpet-bombing. The feed should read as a coherent archive, not a rotating carousel.

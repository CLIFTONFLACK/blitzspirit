# Social copy review — hooks, captions, CTAs

Reviewed: `standalone/src/data/pages/social.json` as served at https://blitz.getbrian.xyz/social
Date: 2026-09-09
Brief: messaging should sound naturally human with an East London edge, simple language,
compelling slogans, built for virality and engagement. The lines should say what people are
thinking but are too scared to say. Reviewed against the Ava content-creation method
(hook archetypes, hook-to-CTA pairing, one CTA per post) and the dossier's own guardrails.

## Verdict

The dossier is well built and well governed. The problem is register, not structure. The
House voice reads like a broadsheet columnist: abstract nouns, Latinate words, no second
person. The Eased voice is stiffer than House because it strips contractions. Neither sounds
like a person from Bow talking to a mate. And the "too scared to say" thread is missing
almost entirely: the lines observe, they never confess.

Locked Brand Book lines are out of scope and are not touched here.

## Findings, by severity

### 1. Eased is more formal than House (systemic, ~40 lines)

Eased is meant to be the warmer register for cold and paid audiences. In practice it drops
every contraction, so it reads like a solicitor's letter.

|---|---|---|
| C-SET-A-01 | It won't be for everyone. That is rather the point. | "rather the point" is Radio 4 |
| C-SET-A-03 | Made because we wanted one. Seems we were not the only ones. | "were not" |
| C-SET-A-04 | You know within a second whether it is your sort of thing. | "it is" |
| C-SET-B-05 | We have joked our way through a great deal worse. | "a great deal" |
| C-SET-D-08 | The design has to stand up on its own before anything else does. | 14 words for a caption |
| A-SET-A-03 | It is in the bio if you want it. | "It is" |
| A-SET-B-01 | It is on a shirt in the bio. | "It is" |
| A-SET-B-06 | It is in the bio, and there will not be a sale. | "there will not be" |

### 3. Hooks: first three words are weak

Ava: the first three words are visible before sound, and must be visceral, specific, or
second person. Groups A, B and C open on abstract nouns.

- "Stubbornness is the..." (H-B-01)
- "Everyone admires grit..." (H-B-02)
- "Heritage isn't a..." (H-B-03)
- "Quiet is not..." (H-B-04)
- "The joke has..." (H-B-05)
- "Around 150,000 a..." (H-C-02)

Group D (POV) and Group E (Question) are the strongest in the dossier because they are
second person by construction. The fix for A/B/C is to start on "you", "your", a name, or
a concrete object.

### 4. The "too scared to say" line is missing

The brief asks for lines that name what people think and don't say. The dossier's Group B
notes "noticing, not arguing", which is right, but the noticing is all safe. Nothing in the
current bank makes a reader think "I've never said that out loud". The best existing
candidates are H-D-06 (apologised to a door) and H-D-03 ("a lot"). There should be a whole
group of these. Proposed Group I below, all inside the guardrails: not political, nobody
recruited, no grievance framing, everyday life only.

### 5. CTAs: "Bio." is not a call to action

Twenty CTAs end in a bare "Bio." On Instagram a cold viewer doesn't decode it. Ava's rule is
one clear action. Several set CTAs also contain no action at all:

| Ref | Current | What it asks the viewer to do |
|---|---|---|
| A-SET-D-01 | You know who this is for. | nothing |
| A-SET-D-05 | She would have worn this one. | nothing |
| A-SET-D-06 | Apologise to no one else. | nothing |
| A-SET-A-04 | Decide in the bio. | unclear |
| A-SET-D-02 | Watch it happen. Bio. | unclear |

Second problem: hook-to-CTA pairing is off for virality. POV is the highest-share format,
and only 3 of 8 POV sets ask for a share or a tag. Listicles should ask for a save; the
dossier has zero save CTAs. Questions should ask for a comment; most do, good.

Fix pattern:
- POV hook -> "Send this to [person]" or "Tag one"
- Question hook -> "Comments" ask, never a link
- Listicle -> "Save it" (a save is a stronger signal than a like)
- Design detail / drop -> keyword DM or "Link in bio", spelled out

### 6. Captions restate the hook

"Written for the sets" captions frequently repeat the hook in other words (C-SET-A-02 vs
H-A-02, C-SET-B-07 vs H-B-07, C-SET-C-04 vs H-C-04). The hook is on screen; the caption is
the second beat. It should add the human detail, the story, or the question. Where the caption
is a restatement, either cut it to nothing or make it the confession.

### 7. Risk items

- **H-C-07 / C-ARC-01: "7 July 2005".** That date is the London bombings. A cold TikTok
  audience will not read it as an origin story; the comments will go one way. The dossier
  already says "use sparingly". Recommend: never as a cold-open hook. Archive and About only,
  with the newspaper story spelled out.
- **V-PROD-EST: "The uniform remains."** "Uniform" is on the banned-word list and the line is
  locked. Open item for the Brand Book, not fixable here.
- **H-F-04 eased: "£25–50"** and **A-LIST-04 eased: "twenty-four hours"** are claims. Already
  tagged, keep them tagged.
- **A-DM-04: "ROLL CALL"** is a two-word keyword. Most comment-trigger tools match on phrase
  fine, but a viewer typing "rollcall" or "roll-call" misses. Add both spellings to the trigger.

## Register rules for the rewrite

East London means rhythm and vocabulary, not phonetic spelling. No "fink", "wiv", "innit".
That reads as parody and the brand is 30 to 60 across class.

Use: you lot, go on then, proper, sort it, have a word, not having it, get on with it, nan,
the old man, cheers, fair enough, mind you, leave it, done, have that, in fairness.
Always contract. Second person by default. Ten words or fewer for a hook. One idea. Nobody
explains the joke.

## Applying this

The page reads overrides from Supabase over the committed JSON. Two routes:

1. Edit `social.json` and rebuild. Permanent, versioned, survives a Revert click.
2. Post overrides through `/api/social` with `op: save`. Live in seconds, reversible line by
   line, capped at 60 edits per request.

Route 1 for the rewrites above. Route 2 for anything still being argued over. New Group I
needs new ids in the JSON, so it is route 1 only.
